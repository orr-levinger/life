# Two Guts, Gills and Lungs Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.
> Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `diet` becomes `grass_gut` and `meat_gut`, `water` becomes `gills` and `lungs`, each organ paid for by
`gut_cost` or `breath_cost`; the owner's running world goes on unchanged at the first step.

**Architecture:** The four genes replace the two everywhere they are read (eating, hitting, water, living, senses,
kinship, saves, the page). A save of before is converted as it loads. The two new seen genes (of the one seen and of the
hitter) are 4 senses at the end (196 → 200), so a brain of before is padded with zero rows at the end, as today.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, NumPy, TensorFlow (the learner), unittest; the page in plain JS and three.js.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-two-guts-design.md` (approved 2026-11-27: "Approve").

## Global Constraints

- Natural values: `gut_cost` 0.15, `breath_cost` 0.15; a save of before gets 0 for both (`TODAY_COSTS`), a new world a
  tenth, as every cost.
- `SEEN_GENES` keeps its 18 places with `meat_gut` for diet and `gills` for water, then `grass_gut` and `lungs`
  (20 in all). The senses write the first 18 where they are today and the last 2 at the end. The species' split
  distance and kinship then count all 20 (a diet difference now weighs in both guts; not measured, watched on the tree).
- No old name left: `grep -rn "\.diet\b\|\"diet\"\|\.water\b\|c.water\|g.water\|g.diet" src tests` finds nothing but
  the terrain's water and the save converter.
- Tests: small hand-made worlds, a step or two; the whole suite under 300 s. Local look: half-size world, browser pane
  closed after.

## Review Focus

- The first step after loading a save of before: same energy, same choices.
- Each organ's worth is a straight line; upkeep is the only thing that makes having both cost more.
