# The New Look Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.
> Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every gene shows clearly on the body and the coat's colour comes from the genes, as the owner's mockups
(round 1 and round 2) show.

**Architecture:** One body maker, `src/static/beasts.js`: `genesOf` reads every gene (lungs, grass gut, each brain
layer, vision reach over its whole range) in tenths; `beastModel` draws the new parts; `coatOf` and a new `markOf` give
the colours; `bodyPicture` frames the whole body. The planet, the Species page, the Make tab and the gallery go on
calling the same functions; the Make tab and species pictures pass each layer's neurons.

**Tech Stack:** JavaScript (three.js), Python 3.12 unittest (the page's files as served).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-new-look-design.md` (approved by the owner, 2026-11-28: "Approve"; the
spec's line on the view file corrected while planning: the world already sends `brain`).

## Global Constraints

- Nothing in the world changes: no rule, no file it writes. Only `src/static/beasts.js`, `src/static/gallery.html`,
  `src/viewer.html` (the Make tab and species pictures pass each layer) and their tests.
- One body maker: every picture and the planet go through `genesOf` → `beastModel`.
- Starts after the Latin names plan (`2026-11-28-species-names.md`) has made its commit: both change `src/viewer.html`.
- Tests: no full life simulation; the whole suite under 300 s. The check world is half size; the browser pane is
  closed after.

## Review Focus

- Bodies with the same rounded genes still share one model (`BEASTS.key`), and far bodies are still drawn in batches
  by plan (`farKey`), so finer steps cost little: measured in Step 4.
- A creature's `brain` (the view file) and the Make tab's neurons are per layer; a species' picture shares its typical
  neurons evenly over its layers.

### Task 1: The new look

**Files:** Modify `src/static/beasts.js`:
- `genesOf(c)`: steps of tenths for every gene; `lungs` and `grass` read (`c.lungs`, `c.grass_gut`); `brain`: each
  layer's neurons over 64 (the starting range's top, `starting_rules()`: neurons 1–64), from `c.brain` (a list) or,
  for a species' typical member, `c.neurons / c.layers` for each of its rounded layers; `reach` from vision reach 1–30.
- `coatOf(g)`: hue by meat gut and gills (green-gold, red, blue), bright by fear, dull by stealth, dark by armour.
  `markOf(g)`: pink by love, gold by kin, violet by learns, pale when all are low.
- `beastModel(g, detail)`: as the round-2 mockup: legs shrink with the gills and are gone at 1, flippers, no neck, a
  fish head with a mouth line and lips, five gill slits; a chest and nostrils by lungs; a belly by grass gut (markings'
  colour); head size by layer 1; a horn tine or crest plate per extra layer, as big as its neurons; bands of bone by
  armour, a shell and helmet at full; a neck frill by kin; ears up to the head's size by hearing; a markings saddle;
  the markings material on belly, frill, saddle and antennae tips; quills, antennae and throat sac bigger.
- `bodyPicture`: the camera placed to fit the body's bounding box (`THREE.Box3`), so no part is cut.
- `PLAIN` (far bodies): `lungs`, `grass` and `brain` added.

Modify `src/static/gallery.html` (rows for lungs, grass gut, each brain layer, armour, kin, hearing, gills; the
coat's colour row); `src/viewer.html` (`drawMadeBody` passes `brain: g.neurons`, the per-layer list, instead of their
sum). Test `tests/test_viewer.py`.

**Interfaces:** `genesOf(c) → {meat, swim, lungs, grass, brain: number[], layers, reach, ...}` (each 0..1 in tenths
but `layers` and `calls`); `coatOf(g) → THREE.Color`; `markOf(g) → THREE.Color`.
